Income Tax Slabs — New Tax Regime (Section 115BAC)

Section Number: 115BAC
Act: Income-tax Act, 2025
...

Section 115BAC, also known as the New Tax Regime, is the default tax 
regime applicable from Assessment Year 2026-27. Under Section 115BAC, 
the income tax slabs for individual taxpayers are as follows:

Income up to Rs 4,00,000: Nil (no tax)
Income from Rs 4,00,001 to Rs 8,00,000: 5%
Income from Rs 8,00,001 to Rs 12,00,000: 10%
Income from Rs 12,00,001 to Rs 16,00,000: 15%
Income from Rs 16,00,001 to Rs 20,00,000: 20%
Income from Rs 20,00,001 to Rs 24,00,000: 25%
Income above Rs 24,00,000: 30%

A standard deduction of Rs 75,000 is available to salaried individuals 
under this regime.

Under Section 87A, a tax rebate of up to Rs 60,000 is available, which 
effectively means individuals with taxable income up to Rs 12,00,000 pay 
no tax after the rebate is applied.

The New Tax Regime is the default regime. Taxpayers who wish to use the 
Old Tax Regime must explicitly opt for it.
