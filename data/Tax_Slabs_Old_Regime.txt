Income Tax Slabs — Old Tax Regime

Act: Income-tax Act, 2025
Tax Year: 2026-27
Source: Union Budget 2026, Press Information Bureau (pib.gov.in)

Under the Old Tax Regime, the income tax slabs for individual taxpayers 
below 60 years of age are as follows:

Income up to Rs 2,50,000: Nil (no tax)
Income from Rs 2,50,001 to Rs 5,00,000: 5%
Income from Rs 5,00,001 to Rs 10,00,000: 20%
Income above Rs 10,00,000: 30%

For senior citizens (aged 60 to 79 years), the basic exemption limit is 
Rs 3,00,000, below which no tax is payable.

For super senior citizens (aged 80 years and above), the basic exemption 
limit is Rs 5,00,000, below which no tax is payable.

A standard deduction of Rs 50,000 is available to salaried individuals 
and pensioners under this regime.

Taxpayers who choose the Old Tax Regime can continue to claim deductions 
such as those under Section 123 (investments), Section 126 (health 
insurance), Section 130 (home loan interest), and other deductions under 
Chapter VIII, which are generally not available under the New Tax Regime.
